Cueing

A teleprompter that follows your voice.

Read your script to the camera at your own pace. The text scrolls with you, waits when you pause, holds its place when you go off script, and finds you again when you skip ahead.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Cueing does not collect, transmit or store your personal data. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics.

What stays on your device

  • Your scripts. Stored only on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Your voice. Speech recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device speech framework. Audio is never uploaded.
  • Your recordings. Video and audio are written to your device and saved to your photo library only when you choose to save them.
  • Session logs. If you turn on the optional developer setting, the app saves recognizer output to your device so a session can be replayed for debugging. These files stay on your device until you delete them or share them yourself.

Permissions

  • Microphone — to follow your place in the script and to record audio.
  • Camera — to record video while you read.
  • Speech recognition — processed on device.
  • Photo library (add only) — to save recordings you export.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Children

Cueing is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
